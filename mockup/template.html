Shipped in this pass

  • ✓Painted biomes with day and dusk

    Seeded terrain, worn roads, themed props, cave and gate, per-biome ambient particles, night lighting with lantern glows. Kingdom Rush, Defense Grid

  • ✓Silhouettes that read the role

    Stone towers that change at each upgrade against round, animated enemies; arcing cannon balls, fletched arrows, frost shards. Defense Grid, PvZ

  • ✓Juice and sound

    Debris, smoke, coins, shake, outlined damage numbers; procedural sound effects and a music bed per biome, with toggles. Juice it or Lose it

  • ✓Early call and telegraphs

    Call the next wave for a bonus that decays over 18 s; spawn banner, road chevrons, wave pips with a final-wave skull. Kingdom Rush, PvZ

  • ✓Paced waves

    Build-up, spike and breather in each 3-wave act; each newcomer arrives alone first with a one-line tip. Nintendo 4-step, Schell

Proposed next

  1. 1HUD overlaid on the map

    Wooden plaques in the corners free the side panel, so the board grows to the full width on every screen.

  2. 2Boss bar

    Name and health at the top whenever a boss is on the road, with its own horn and slow motion on the kill.

  3. 3Early-call flag on the map

    The countdown ring sits on the cave itself; tapping it calls the wave and shows the bonus shrinking live.

  4. 4Radial build menu

    Tap a tile, pick from four portraits around it; unaffordable towers grey out. One tap fewer than the side shop and easier with a thumb.

  5. 5A hero with a rally point

    One controllable unit that blocks the road and levels up across the campaign, so the player stays busy between builds.

  6. 6Powers as round cooldown dials

    Readable at a glance in the corner; calling a wave early also shortens their cooldowns.
